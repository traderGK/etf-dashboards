The break-even win rate is the hit rate a given reward:risk needs just to stop losing money — before commissions, slippage and taxes, all of which move it against you. Kelly is shown because it's the mathematically optimal growth fraction, and half-Kelly is shown because full Kelly's drawdowns are unlivable for actual humans. Note the last cell above: a 20% drawdown needs a 25% gain to recover, and a 50% drawdown needs 100%. That asymmetry is why risk management outranks entry selection.
