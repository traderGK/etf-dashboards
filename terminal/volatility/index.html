Term structure

Contango — 9-day vol at 12.8 vs 3-month at 17.9 (ratio 0.71)
An upward-sloping curve is the normal state: near-term calm priced below far-dated uncertainty. The inversion of this ratio through 1.0 is the single cleanest "regime is breaking" signal in the vol space — it usually flips before price confirms.

Implied vs realized

▬ VIX (what options price) · ▬ 21-day realized vol (what the market delivered) · 12 months. The gap is the variance risk premium — persistently positive because insurance costs money. Extremes are the signal: a huge gap = fear is overpaid; realized ABOVE implied = the market is under-hedged for what is already happening.
