METHODOLOGY & HONESTY
Confluence is context, not a signal service. The stance directions are deliberately coarse — bullish, neutral, bearish — because pretending to more precision than the inputs carry is exactly how black boxes lie. The transparency is the product: every read, its source page, and its weight are on this screen.

Known limits, stated plainly. The signals are not fully independent — momentum and relative strength share DNA, so their agreement is worth less than agreement between, say, valuation and credit. Weights are judgement, not optimisation. And the slow signals will disagree with the fast ones at every genuine turning point; that disagreement is the information, not a bug to be averaged away. This page exists to stop you cherry-picking the modules that happen to agree with the position you already wanted to take.
