HOW TO READ IT
Green moving-average cells = price above that average (trend support below); red = trend overhead as resistance. The 52-week extremes are the market's memory — approaches of the high with strong breadth break through; with weak breadth they get sold. Pivot = (1m high + low + close) / 3, a mean-reversion magnet for the coming weeks.
