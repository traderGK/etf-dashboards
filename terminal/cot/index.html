HOW TO READ IT
  • Commercials — producers and hedgers offsetting real-world exposure. They fade trends (sell strength, buy weakness) and tend to be early. Treat them as the informed side.
  • Large speculators — funds and managed money, trend-followers. Their extremes mark crowded trades where the fuel is already spent.
  • Small traders — below reporting size; historically most wrong at turning points.
  • COT index — where the commercials' net sits inside its 26-week range: ≥80 means they're as long as they've been in six months (⚡ contrarian-bullish zone), ≤20 the opposite. The ★ flag marks the strongest setup: commercials at one extreme while large specs sit at the other.
  • 26w trend — sparkline of the commercials' net position; the slope matters more than the level.
