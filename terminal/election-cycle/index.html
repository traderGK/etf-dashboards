WHERE WE ARE
2026 is a midterm year (averages +2.8%). The classic pattern: midterm years run weak and choppy into autumn, then launch the strongest 12-month window of the cycle; pre-election years are historically the best full year. The mechanism is policy — administrations stimulate into re-election. Treat it as context, not destiny: single cycles deviate wildly.
