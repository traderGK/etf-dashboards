HOW TO TRADE THE QUADRANTS
  • The cycle is clockwise: strength builds (Improving, top-left), peaks (Leading, top-right), fades (Weakening, bottom-right), bottoms (Lagging, bottom-left) — then repeats. Where a name IS matters less than where its trail is HEADED.
  • Improving is where alpha is born — relative strength has turned before the crowd repriced it. Names freshly arrived from Lagging (the NEW badge) are the highest risk/reward entries in rotation strategy.
  • Leading is for holding, not chasing — buy pullbacks, and treat a south-pointing arrow as the start of distribution even while price still looks fine.
  • Weakening is the exit ramp — still outperforming on trend, but the money is already leaving. Most traders overstay here.
  • Lagging is only interesting when its arrow points north — that's next quarter's Improving story, a watchlist entry rather than a position.
RS-Ratio = the asset/benchmark ratio versus its own 14-week average (×100); RS-Momentum = the same normalization applied to RS-Ratio. Both center on 100 — the crosshair — computed on weekly closes with 8-week trails.
