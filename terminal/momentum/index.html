THE TWO FILTERS THAT MATTER
The classic 10-month moving-average filter on the S&P is currently RISK-ON (price above). It's a blunt tool that whipsaws in chop, but it has kept investors out of every major bear market for a century. 12-1 momentum (last year excluding the latest month) is the academic standard: own what's already working, skip the most recent month to dodge mean-reversion.
