METHODOLOGY
  • Score (0–100) = 30% trend structure (price vs the 20/50/200-day averages and their stacking) + 20% RSI(14) + 20% one-month rate-of-change percentile vs the asset's own year + 15% 52-week range position + 15% MACD(12,26,9) state.
  • TF align — the four blocks are today / 1-week / 1-month / 3-month direction. Four green = every horizon pushing the same way; mixed = chop, where momentum styles lose their edge.
  • Flags — ▲ the 5-day pace is outrunning the month (freshest momentum); ▼ the week is fading versus the month; ◆ a fresh 20-day price extreme that RSI refused to confirm.
  • Use the spread — pairing the strongest against the weakest name inside a group is the cleanest relative-value expression of this table.
