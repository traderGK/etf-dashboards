HOW TO USE IT
  • Fade the extremes, not the middle — sub-25 and 75+ are the actionable zones; a 55 is noise, a 15 into a seasonally strong month is a signal.
  • Read the strip for the trend of mood — 20 today from 60 a month ago is capitulation in progress; 20 that has sat at 20 for a quarter is a bear regime, not a bottom.
  • Cross the markets — equity greed against crypto fear is a rotation tell; synchronized extreme fear on all four cards is the washout signature that marks cycle lows.
  • Confirm with funding — crypto fear plus negative perp funding means the crowd is short AND scared: the highest-octane squeeze setup there is.
Construction: the equities gauge rebuilds five classic fear/greed inputs from raw data (index momentum vs its 125-day average, VIX level, VIX term structure, 20-day stock-vs-bond demand, junk-bond demand) as rolling one-year percentiles, averaged. Crypto uses the alternative.me industry-standard index. Gold and the dollar have no credible survey, so those cards use an RSI + momentum-percentile proxy — labelled, not disguised.
