Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information TraderGK (“we”, “us”) collects when you use tradergk.com, how we use it, and who we share it with. We collect as little as possible and never sell your data.

1Information we collect

  • Email address you provide at checkout — used to deliver your access key and to provide support.
  • Name, if you choose to provide it at checkout.
  • Payment information — handled entirely by our payment processors (see §3). We do not receive or store your full card number or banking details.
  • Access-key data — your key and its activation and expiry status, so we can grant and manage access.
  • Technical / log data — IP address, browser type, and timestamps, collected as standard server logs for security and abuse-prevention.
  • Local browser storage — the site stores your access key and cached dashboard data in your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in and load quickly. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

2How we use it

We use this information to deliver and manage your access, provide customer support, prevent fraud and abuse, comply with legal and accounting obligations, and improve the Service. We do not use it for third-party advertising.

3Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers strictly as needed to operate the Service:

  • Lemon Squeezy — card payments and Merchant of Record (billing, receipts, tax). See Lemon Squeezy’s own privacy policy.
  • Crypto payment processor — where you choose to pay with cryptocurrency.
  • Cloudflare — hosting, security, and the server-side access gate.
  • GitHub Pages — static website hosting.
  • Email delivery provider — to send your access key and support messages.

4Data retention

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above and to meet legal or accounting requirements, after which it is deleted or anonymized. You may request earlier deletion (see §6).

5International processing

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

6Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, email support@tradergk.com and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

7Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including a server-side access gate. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8Children

The Service is not directed to individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted, indicated by the “Last updated” date above.

10Contact

Questions about your privacy? Contact us at support@tradergk.com.
