Refund Policy

Last updated: 17 June 2026

TraderGK sells a digital product — an educational market-research access pass that is delivered instantly. This policy explains when refunds are available. We want every customer to be treated fairly.

Full refund for unused keys within 14 days. If you have not activated or used your access key, you may request a full refund within 14 days of purchase — no questions asked.

1Nature of the product

Access is sold as a fixed 7-day pass for $500 USD, delivered immediately as a single access key that unlocks educational content. Because the content is digital and available the moment your key is activated, the rules below apply.

2Unused access keys

If your key has not been activated, you are entitled to a full refund within 14 days of purchase. Simply contact us with your order details.

3Activated access keys

Once your access key has been activated or used, the educational content has been delivered and accessed, and the purchase is non-refundable. Access is for information, not for investment results, and refunds are not granted based on market outcomes or trading performance.

4How to request a refund

Email support@tradergk.com with the email address used at checkout and your order or receipt number.

  • Card payments: processed by Lemon Squeezy (our Merchant of Record); approved refunds are returned to your original payment method, typically within 5–10 business days.
  • Crypto payments: approved refunds, where applicable, are returned to the originating wallet net of network fees. We are not responsible for cryptocurrency value fluctuations between purchase and refund.

5Technical issues & duplicate charges

If you were charged in error, charged more than once, or a technical fault prevented you from accessing the Service, contact us and we will investigate and make it right, including a full refund where appropriate.

6Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is incorrect, please contact us first — we resolve almost everything quickly. Filing a chargeback without contacting us may result in your access being revoked, and fraudulent chargebacks may be disputed.

7Contact

Questions about a refund? Contact us at support@tradergk.com.
